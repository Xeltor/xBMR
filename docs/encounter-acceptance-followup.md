## Applying the maturity policy

not promise a complete automatic solver or live AI proof. Personal play, exact
server snapshots, pristine holdouts and hypothetical unseen cancellations are not
independent gates. Known unsupported routes, unsafe boundaries or missing reliable
responses remain material gaps. `AISupport` requires additional live AI evidence.
