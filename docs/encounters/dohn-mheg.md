# Dohn Mheg

The normal level-73 dungeon guides agree that players should intercept distinct Fodder beams and destroy their own Painted Root when Coiling Ivy binds them. The wiki calls the bind adds Painted Ivy. The observed game objects and Thonky call them Painted Roots. The guides describe the mechanic and player response without specifying the beam width or the server snapshot.

## Assignment and timing

The previous module selected a sapling by party slot and treated the last sapling creation as a common deadline. A companion could intercept the selected beam while the player's movement still aimed for it. The revised component reserves beams according to their recorded tether recipients, keeps an intercepted beam assigned to its player until it resolves, and assigns distinct free beams to the remaining players in party order. It guides the player as soon as a relevant tether appears. Trust companions choose their own beams. The radar draws the actual lines and highlights the player's assigned line. This is a simultaneous assignment without a sequence of future hazards. Feeding Time, untethering and despawn retire the corresponding movement constraint.

The corridor retains the module's 0.5-yalm half width and starts just outside Griaule's current hitbox. Each sapling keeps its own inherited 10.9-second spawn-to-feed estimate across tether transfers. Exact width, server snapshot and last-safe departure remain unverified. Swinge remains a separate timed hazard. Coiling Ivy warns from the cast, gives the nearby live root first target priority while the player is fettered, and pauses automated walking and dash planning during the bind. Fodder becomes actionable again on the first frame without Fetters. Root proximity identifies the likely owner when the status has no usable source. After release, all remaining roots retain priority over the boss.

The reported Trust failure reopened the module's Fodder assignment for review. The focused regression covers availability and geometry of the native movement hint, Trust interception, distinct player assignments, resolution, respawn and root targeting. A live clear with the changed build requires a fresh pull.

## Lakelord's tightrope and Liar's Lyre

The pinned upstream BMR revision and the released xBMR use identical Dohn Mheg encounter modules. The two guides describe walking the tightrope, entering the barrier and destroying the Liar's Lyre before Finale. The wiki describes the penalty and the Trust companions' scripted movement. The existing bridge polygon and damage-eligibility distance were retained.

In the live combat diagnostics, the alt reached the east bend at approximately (-115.26, -244.64) and stayed there from 19:40:49 to 19:40:59 local time. On consecutive frames, xBMR found a complete native route with score SafeMaxPrio to (-128.00, -258.48) but issued Wait while it had no eligible offensive target or selected action. The optimizer treated fresh native route leeway as permission to defer the walk. At the recorded position, the native map produces the same destination and complete witness offline. The optimizer's stricter ordinary-walk check rejects that witness, so merely preferring the route inside the optimizer still leaves the player waiting. The chasm phase requests the existing native steering path, with the usual native movement authority, obstacle checks and physical movement probes. It publishes the dash restriction and promotes the Lyre once inside the existing barrier-distance gate. The native map and route still use the upstream bridge polygon. Finale ending clears the phase's steering request.

The first boss's retained replay shows no recorded geyser or Hydrofall hits on the alt. The alt joined Laughing Leap on Urianger. The Trust tank was outside the stack. In the completed replay, three Imp Choir effects (258987, 279279 and 285026) had no recorded transformation effect or matching status gain on the alt. Neither observed Toad Choir listed a hit. The replay alone cannot attribute these outcomes to automated facing or movement. Those mechanics retain their existing handling. A full unattended AutoDuty run on the candidate is pending.
