# Walking progress and useful healing contact

The maintainer reported walking-trash hesitation followed by late gaps, and
healers standing far from useful group-heal coverage on 2026-10-25. Live current
diagnostics showed repeated native waiting while optional optimization consumed
the native freshness window. A long single-target healing alternative could
also satisfy configured healing access without providing group-heal posture.

The existing native ThetaStar now proposes an ordinary walking corridor toward
missing combat range or party contact before full mobility enumeration. The same
joint search models current actions, recipients, locks, travel and assignments
inside that corridor. Every publication is fully revalidated on the original
map and explicitly bounded; it is not exhaustive optimization. Original capture,
freshness and departure clocks are never renewed. Forced displacement, teleports,
field-placement proposals and already-selected mobility retain their full path.
Optional utility/mobility actions cannot delay this walking proposal. The full
search remains responsible for comparing useful legal mobility afterward.

Enabled healer coverage now also supplies a combat-only preparatory party-contact
region from saved enabled, unlocked radial healing footprints. RSR's GCD-heal
opt-out excludes those spells. Every living observed party recipient must fit
the shared footprint; the preference stays closer to its center with spare
reach. This does not claim a heal is selected, off cooldown or delivered, change
its actual range, select a new follow leader or enable between-pull following.
Mechanics, current casts/actions, manual control, native assignments and the
saved movement/healing-coverage opt-outs remain authoritative.

The native regression suite covers early walking publication, exact future-cast
delivery with movement during readiness, actual cast locks, contact recovery
while offensive range is already satisfied, physical obstruction invalidation
and shared radial coverage. Build and synthetic timing success remain separate
from live acceptance of smooth walking and healer positioning.
