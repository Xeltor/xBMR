# Return to our own Earthly Star

The maintainer reported missing movement into the player's own Earthly Star on
2026-10-25. Capture previously excluded the star while the player was at full HP
and had no forecast incoming damage. Generic range/contact preferences also ranked
ahead of the ordinary friendly-zone benefit.

An observed star owned by the local AST now remains a movement objective before
HP falls. Among complete safe routes preserving current actions, recipients,
native goal priority and configured healing access, walking into our own star
ranks ahead of generic combat range, party contact and cosmetic continuity. This
preference does not spend mobility resources. Range/placement pruning no longer
assumes those lower objectives settle an active own-star comparison.

The existing 20-yalm radius and original 20-second placement lifetime match the
[official AST guide](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/jobguide/astrologian/): the first
ten seconds mature into Giant Dominance for ten more seconds. Maturation does
not renew the lifetime. Observed detonation, expiry, owner removal and zone
changes retire the captured star. Placement coordinates come from native cast
events; no location is fabricated from the owner's status or current position.
An existing star whose placement predates plugin initialization is not recovered
from status alone.

The defensive-ground positioning opt-out, activation/manual gates, arena bounds,
independent assignments and hazard timing remain authoritative. Other players'
stars retain the existing personal healing-need gate and ordinary zone priority.
Regressions cover healthy own-star capture, maturity/expiry, early detonation,
opt-outs, competing range/contact, current cast preservation, native goals,
blocked/assigned destinations, fresh revalidation and resource preservation.
Build/replay success does not establish live fight acceptance.
